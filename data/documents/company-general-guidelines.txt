Company Knowledge Management Guidelines

All employees should follow the company's standard knowledge management practices to ensure that information is accurate, accessible, and up to date.

Documents should be stored in the approved company repositories, such as SharePoint. Each document should include relevant metadata, including title, department, region, document owner, and last updated date.

Employees should verify information before sharing it with clients or other teams. Outdated documents should be reviewed and updated by the responsible document owner.

When a team member cannot find the required information, they should contact the relevant department or knowledge management team for assistance.

Sensitive or confidential information must only be accessed by authorized employees. Users should not share restricted documents with people who do not have the required permissions.

The knowledge management team is responsible for maintaining document organization, improving search capabilities, and helping employees locate relevant information efficiently.
