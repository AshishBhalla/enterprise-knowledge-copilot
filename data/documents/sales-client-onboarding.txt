Title: Client Onboarding Guidelines

Department: Sales

Region: Global

Document Type: Process Guide

Owner: Sales Operations

The client onboarding process helps sales teams ensure that new clients are introduced to the appropriate service teams and operational contacts.

The process includes the following steps:

1. Collect client requirements and business objectives.
2. Identify the relevant service offerings and subject matter experts.
3. Validate the proposed solution with the delivery team.
4. Prepare the client onboarding documentation.
5. Schedule an introduction between the client and the service team.

Sales teams should confirm that the required documentation has been reviewed before the client onboarding meeting.

For complex client requirements, the sales team should involve the relevant regional or practice specialists.
