Title: Policy Renewal Process

Department: Operations

Region: Australia

Document Type: Process Guide

Owner: Operations Team

The policy renewal process helps the operations team review existing policies and coordinate renewal activities with clients and insurers.

The process generally includes:

1. Review the existing policy details.
2. Identify upcoming renewal dates.
3. Collect updated client information.
4. Review coverage requirements and changes.
5. Coordinate renewal quotations with insurers.
6. Share the renewal options with the client.
7. Record the final renewal decision.

The operations team should ensure that important client information is validated before renewal documentation is prepared.

Any exceptions or unusual client requirements should be escalated to the appropriate operations manager for review.
